What is Your Company AI?

It is your company's own AI app, published on the App Store and Google Play with your name and logo. It knows your products, prices and services, and answers customers instantly, like a sales representative who works 24/7. The technology is powered by DigitalOman.ai.

Does the app really work without internet?

Yes. The AI runs on the customer's phone, so once the app is installed it can answer questions with no internet connection: in the desert, in the mountains or on a flight. New products and prices reach customers the next time their phone is online and the app updates.

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay once for your AI app, with no monthly subscription and no per-chat charges from us. Apple and Google set their own developer account fees for publishing on their stores, and we explain these clearly during your demo.

How is it different from a website or a chatbot?

A website waits for customers to find it, cannot hold a conversation and stops working without internet. Most chatbots live inside a website and charge monthly fees. Your AI app sits on the customer's phone, carries your brand on the app stores, answers questions instantly and keeps working offline.

Will the app use my company name and logo?

Yes. The app is published with your company name, logo and colours, so customers see your brand on the App Store, Google Play and their home screen.

Which businesses is it for?

Any business whose customers ask questions before they buy: perfume and oud shops, furniture and décor, electronics, restaurants and cafés, real estate, car dealers, clinics, and retail and fashion stores across Oman.

Can I update my products and prices later?

Yes. Send us your updated catalogue or prices and we publish an updated version of your app, so customers always get the latest answers.

Is customer data private?

Yes. Because the AI runs on the customer's own phone, conversations stay on their device instead of being sent to a server.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on the size of your catalogue and the app store review process. We confirm a clear timeline during your free demo.
